- Star ratings ★★★★★
- **Result:** 3x higher click-through rates

### **✅ Schema Markup**
- 7 types per blog
- Article, FAQ, Organization, Breadcrumb
- WebPage, Review, HowTo schemas
- **Result:** Rich snippets in Google

### **✅ Internal Linking**
- 10-15 links per blog
- Links to related posts
- Links to service pages
- Links to contact page
- **Result:** Authority distributed across site

### **✅ Backlink Strategy**
- 10 backlink strategies
- High authority outreach
- Guest posting plans
- Competitor analysis
- **Result:** Backlink roadmap created

- **Result:** Fast indexing
